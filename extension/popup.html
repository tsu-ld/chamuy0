Two quick steps and you are done. Your API key and your training labels never leave this browser profile.

Step 1. Grant access

The extension needs permission to read your LinkedIn feed and to call the TypeSafe API. Click once and accept the browser prompt.

Prompt closes before you can click it?

Some Firefox builds, Zen included, dismiss the permission prompt in about a second. Grant it by hand instead:

  1. Open a new tab and go to about:addons.
  2. Click chamuy0, then the Permissions tab.
  3. Turn on access for www.linkedin.com and api.typesafe.ai.

Then reopen this popup and reload linkedin.com.

Step 2. TypeSafe API key

Paste your key from the TypeSafe console. Save, then open or reload linkedin.com.
